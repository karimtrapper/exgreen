Frequently asked questions

How does exchange via Grusha work?

You message us on Telegram or WhatsApp, we lock the rate and agree on the details. You send rubles via SBP or USDT to our wallet. Once confirmed, you receive baht — to a Thai bank card, in cash via courier, via ATM by QR-code, or at our Phuket office. The whole process takes from 15 minutes.

Is it safe? What license do you hold?

Yes. We operate through a Thai company (Co., Ltd) with Bank of Thailand license MC225660186. The license is verifiable on the Bank of Thailand website. All payments go through official banking channels with confirmations.

What are the minimum and maximum exchange amounts?

Minimum is 50,000 RUB or 500 USDT. There is no fixed maximum — we have processed deals above 1,000,000 THB. For large amounts we negotiate an individual rate.

Do I need a passport or any documents?

No documents are required for a standard exchange. For property developer invoice payments a copy of the contract may be needed — it depends on the receiving bank.

What exchange directions are available?

Main directions: RUB → THB, USDT → THB, THB → USDT, RUB → USDT. The rate updates in real time and is locked at the moment of the deal.

How quickly will I receive the money?

Cash via courier or at the office — 15 to 40 minutes after payment confirmation. Transfer to a Thai bank account — same day. Developer invoice payments — 1–2 business days.

Where is your office?

Our office is located at 48/207 Choeng Thale, Thalang, Phuket 83110. Open daily from 09:00 to 21:00. Drop in for cash exchange or an in-person meeting.
